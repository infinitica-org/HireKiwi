# HireKiwi — Security alerts and runbooks

> Owner: Vishal V · Ticket: S6-VV-126 (security-critical failures, Zoho #574) · Rules: `infra/observability/grafana/provisioning/alerting/hirekiwi-security-alerts.yml`

Security alerts carry `category=security` and go through the same **hirekiwi-team** contact point as the platform
alerts (S6-VV-125). They read two metrics:

- `hirekiwi_security_events_total{action, reason}`, counted where the event is audited (`AuditPublisherService`) for
  `auth.login_failed` (with its reason), `auth.account_locked`, `auth.refresh_reuse_detected`,
  `support.access_granted` and `user.role_changed`.
- `hirekiwi_authz_denied_total{route, role}`, counted when the role/permission guard refuses a request.

For every alert, the audit log is the source of truth: `/admin/audit-logs?action=<action>` lists who, what and when.

## Critical

### refresh-reuse
A revoked refresh token was used again. HireKiwi already revoked the whole token family, so the attacker and the real
user are both signed out. Find the user in the audit log (`auth.refresh_reuse_detected`), check their recent logins
and IPs, and contact them if it wasn't a stale tab. Repeated hits on one account mean the token store or a device is
compromised.

### login-failure-burst
More than 50 failed logins in 10 minutes. Split by reason in Grafana (`sum by (reason)`): mostly `unknown_email`
means credential stuffing against a list; mostly `bad_password` on few accounts means targeted guessing (lockouts
will follow). Check the rate limiter is rejecting (`hirekiwi_rate_limit_rejections_total`) and block the source at Caddy
if one range dominates.

### infected-upload
The malware scanner rejected a file. The upload was refused and nothing was stored. Find the uploader from the
logs at that time and check whether their other uploads or account look abusive.

## Warning

### lockouts
More than 5 accounts locked in 15 minutes. Usually the tail of a login-failure burst; if it isn't, check whether a
client (an old app build, a bad integration) is retrying with wrong credentials.

### authz-denied-spike
More than 100 refused requests in 10 minutes. Group by `route` and `role`: one role hammering admin routes is
probing; many roles on one route after a deploy is a permissions bug in that release.

### scanner-errors
The scanner errored on several uploads. Uploads fail closed while it's down, so users can't upload documents. Check
the `clamav` service and its signature updates.

### support-access
A support agent got time-limited access to a user account. Confirm the grant matches a support ticket (`ticketId`
in the audit row). Unexplained grants go to the security owner.

### role-change
An institution staff role changed. Confirm the actor and reason in the audit log; an unexpected promotion to admin
is an incident.
