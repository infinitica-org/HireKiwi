Welcome to **SmartKiwi (SMART)**.

SmartKiwi is an **evidence-backed intelligent talent discovery platform** that transforms fragmented information about people into **evidence-backed capability profiles**. It brings together evidence from multiple active and passive sources, verifies and interprets that evidence, and creates a richer representation of a person's skills, capabilities, experience, and potential.

This section provides the foundational understanding required to work with SmartKiwi.

---

## 00.1 What is SmartKiwi?

**SmartKiwi is an evidence-backed intelligent talent discovery platform.**

Traditional talent platforms primarily depend on self-declared resumes, profiles, job titles, and keywords. SmartKiwi takes a different approach.

SmartKiwi builds a continuously evolving representation of talent by bringing together **active and passive evidence** from multiple sources.

### Active Evidence

Evidence generated through a person's direct participation or demonstrated performance.

Examples include:

- Criterion-referenced assessments (L1 MCQ, L2 Code/SQL Sandbox)
- Spoken BARS evaluations (L3)
- AI-assisted project defense simulations (L4)
- Capstone evaluations (L5)
- Structured role interviews & simulations
- Directly observed activities

### Passive Evidence

Evidence that already exists outside a specific assessment attempt.

Examples include:

- Work experience & internships
- Open-source & collaborative projects (GitHub/GitLab)
- Coding track records (LeetCode/HackerRank)
- Verified university degrees & SIS records
- Authoritative certifications (AWS, GCP, Credly)
- Endorsements from verified corporate managers
- Portfolios, publications, and technical achievements

SmartKiwi combines these evidence sources to create a complete, defensible understanding of an individual's capabilities.

### The Core Idea

SmartKiwi does not simply ask:

> "What does this person say they can do?"

It aims to answer:

> **"What evidence exists that demonstrates what this person can do?"**

The result is an **evidence-backed capability profile** that continuously evolves as new evidence is added, verified, and interpreted.

## 00.4 What SmartKiwi Does

SmartKiwi performs five fundamental functions:

### 1. Collects Evidence (Active + Passive)

SmartKiwi gathers evidence from multiple active and passive sources:

- **Active sources:** Multi-tier assessments (L1–L5), interactive project defenses, spoken evaluations, technical exercises.
- **Passive sources:** GitHub/GitLab repositories, LeetCode performance, corporate work experience, verified certifications, university academic records, manager endorsements.

### 2. Organizes Evidence via Universal Capability Ontology

SmartKiwi structures fragmented information into a unified capability record linked to a canonical ontology:

- Domains & Competencies
- Canonical Skills & Tools/Technologies
- Knowledge areas & Tasks
- Standardized taxonomies (ESCO, O*NET)

### 3. Verifies Evidence & Applies Dual Human Review

Verification in SmartKiwi is executed through modular, automated verification pipelines and clear human-review governance:

- **Academic Verification at Source:** Direct queries against institutional SIS/ERP data and corporate domain whitelists.
- **Code & AST Provenance at Source:** Direct connection to GitHub/GitLab APIs to evaluate commit author signatures, repository fork trees, and AST complexity.
- **Corporate Experience at Source:** Cryptographically signed, single-use expiring token links dispatched exclusively to corporate email domains (`@company.com`), detecting circular endorsement fraud rings.
- **Credentials at Source:** Direct API verification against credential issuing authorities (AWS, Credly, Google Cloud, Coursera).
- **Dual Human Review Accountability:**
  - *Partnered Institution Students:* Academic integrity flags and verification disputes are routed to the **College / Placement Admin Queue**.
  - *Independent Candidates:* Flagged cases are routed to the **SmartKiwi Platform Trust & Safety Queue**.

Verification produces tamper-evident, HMAC-SHA256 signed evidence nodes with standardized Verification IDs and confidence ratings.

### 4. Builds Talent Intelligence (Evidence Fusion & Inference Engine)

SmartKiwi interprets evidence to create a richer representation of an individual's capabilities:

- Skill & Competency Proficiency Levels (1–5 scale)
- Calibrated Evidence Confidence Scores
- Evidence Breakdown (Assessed vs. Inferred vs. Claimed)
- Identified Skill Gaps & Actionable Assessment Recommendations
- Deep Learning & 4D Telemetry Validation (ensuring reliability and eliminating hallucination)

### 5. Algorithmic Candidate-to-Role Matching

Once a person's capabilities are structured, SmartKiwi connects them with relevant opportunities using **deterministic, computationally efficient matching**:

- SQL-level hard eligibility constraints
- Weighted role requirement scoring against proficiency thresholds
- `pgvector` HNSW cosine similarity for semantic candidate-to-role requirement embeddings
- Transparent, inspectable match explanations (no black-box LLM ranking)
- Batch-normalized scoring across candidate batches
- Event-driven recomputation upon profile upgrades or new student registrations

## 00.5 What SmartKiwi Does Not Do

Understanding what SmartKiwi does **not** do is critical for maintaining product focus:

- **SmartKiwi is not simply a resume builder:** Resumes represent self-reported claims. SmartKiwi builds an evidence-backed intelligence graph.
- **SmartKiwi is not simply an assessment platform:** Assessments are just one active evidence stream feeding into the wider talent graph.
- **SmartKiwi is not simply a job board:** Connecting opportunities is a product of high-fidelity capability matching, not a classified listing service.
- **SmartKiwi is not simply a student profile platform:** While starting with campus placements, the architecture supports lifelong workforce mobility.
- **SmartKiwi is not a replacement for human hiring decisions:** SmartKiwi provides verified evidence and explainable match scores. Final hiring decisions always belong to humans.
- **SmartKiwi does not treat every piece of information as equally trustworthy:** Unverified claims are strictly segregated from cryptographically signed, demonstrated proof.
- **SmartKiwi does not reduce a person to a single number:** Competencies are multi-dimensional across cognitive, technical, and behavioral domains.

## 00.6 Product Principles

```
Claim ──► Evidence ──► Verification ──► Intelligence ──► Opportunity
```

1. **Evidence Over Claims:** Capability must be demonstrated and verified, not merely asserted.
2. **Multiple Signals Over a Single Source:** Active evaluations and passive history combine to eliminate single-test bias.
3. **Context Matters:** A skill is evaluated within its domain, recency, depth, and project context.
4. **Transparency Over Black Boxes:** Every match, score, and inference must have human-understandable explanations.
5. **Human Agency:** Empower talent and employers with verifiable data; never automate life-altering rejections arbitrarily.
6. **Continuous Profile Evolution:** Profiles adapt dynamically as new projects, assessments, and experiences are completed.
7. **Trust Must Be Earned:** Clear boundaries between claimed, supported, verified, and demonstrated states.
8. **Separate Evidence From Interpretation:** Raw observations (code diffs, test logs) remain immutable and distinct from AI/human inferences.
9. **Explainability Is a Product Requirement:** System inferences must reveal their underlying evidence triggers.
10. **Privacy by Design:** Strict adherence to data minimization, explicit consent, and DPDP Act 2023 regulations.
11. **Build for the Network:** Value must compound mutually for students, institutions, and enterprise employers.
12. **Capability Over Credentials:** What a candidate can actually build and solve outweighs brand-name pedigree alone.

## 00.7 SmartKiwi Terminology & Glossary

| Term | Enterprise Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SmartKiwi (SMART)** | The evidence-backed intelligent talent discovery platform. |
| **Verification Engine** | Automated ingestion pipelines and cryptographic validators verifying data at source. |
| **Human Review Queue** | Dual accountability review: College/TPO for partnered students, Platform Trust & Safety for independent candidates. |

| **Inference Engine** | Probabilistic and rule-based models estimating proficiency levels (1–5) and confidence scores from evidence. |
| **Algorithmic Matching** | Deterministic, computationally efficient candidate-to-role matching using constraints, weights, and pgvector cosine similarity. |
| **Verification ID** | Canonical cryptographic hash and URL confirming authenticity of credentials. |
| **Confidence Note** | Empirical calibration report (sample size, Cronbach's α, source verification status) appended to every profile. |
