## 12.1 University Platform Overview

The University Platform (`tpo.becomesmart.online`) serves as the administrative mission control for campus Training and Placement Officers (TPOs), Deans, and department placement coordinators.

```
[Student Provisioning] ──► [Cohort Skill Heatmap] ──► [Host Recruiter Drives] ──► [NAAC/NIRF Reports]
```

---

## 12.2 Institutional Tenancy & Student Provisioning

- **Multi-Campus Hierarchy:** Supports multi-campus university systems with distinct department branches (Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics).
- **Automated Roster Provisioning:** Bulk CSV ingestion or REST API integration with campus ERPs (Ellucian, SAP, Jenzabar, Canvas LMS).
- **Eligibility Rules Enforcement:** TPOs define institutional placement policies (e.g., maximum 2 active backlogs allowed, minimum $60\%$ aggregate academic score).

---

## 12.3 Cohort Readiness & Department Heatmaps

The TPO dashboard visualizes aggregate talent readiness across the student body:

- **Department Competency Heatmap:** Identifies systemic curriculum strengths and weaknesses (e.g., _"Department of CSE: $74\%$ Gold in Frontend, but only $28\%$ Silver in Cloud & DevOps"_).
- **Intervention Flags:** Highlights capable students who have not completed baseline project defenses, enabling targeted placement counseling.
- **Industry Readiness Index:** Benchmarks the university's graduating batch against national engineering cohort percentiles.

---

## 12.4 Campus Placement Drive Operations

TPOs manage on-campus corporate recruitment drives end-to-end:

1. **Drive Creation:** TPO invites corporate partners or reviews incoming recruiter applications.
2. **Automated Shortlisting:** System applies company criteria (e.g., B.Tech CS + 7.5 CGPA + Silver Tier Backend) and generates an instant, audited candidate shortlist.
3. **Slot & Schedule Management:** Allocates interview slots and tracks student offer acceptances in accordance with campus "One Student, One Job" rules.

---

## 12.5 Regulatory Placement Reporting (NAAC / NIRF / NBA)

SmartKiwi automates statutory accreditation reporting:

- **One-Click Export:** Generates standardized spreadsheets with verified student names, roll numbers, hiring companies, packages (CTC), and offer verification hashes.
- **Zero Discrepancies:** All reported offers are cryptographically linked to corporate employer verification tokens, eliminating audit discrepancies.
