## 25.1 System Purpose & Scope Definition

HireKiwi is an AI-powered **evidence-backed talent network platform** that connects universities, students, and enterprise employers around verified, evidence-based capabilities rather than self-declared resume claims (Source of Truth: `smart.ai_PRD_Functional_and_Non-Functional_Requirements.xlsx`, synthesized from the 442-story product backlog across 52 modules).

### The Core Problem
- **Employers:** Cannot trust self-declared student CVs or inflated keyword claims; spend 40+ hours per role on manual screening with high false-positive rates.
- **Students:** Lack mechanisms to demonstrate genuine problem-solving capabilities beyond college pedigree and GPA.
- **Universities:** Lack real-time visibility into student placement readiness and curriculum deficits.

### The HireKiwi Solution
HireKiwi collects multi-signal evidence (academic SIS records, GitHub repositories, corporate internship endorsements, and proctored technical evaluations), deploys autonomous agents led by **Vivi** (Source Verification Agent) to interrogate evidence directly at its authentic source, infers explainable skill levels with calibrated confidence, and matches students to active jobs with transparent reasons.

## 25.4 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

In accordance with the PRD `Non-Functional Reqs` baseline:

### 1. Performance SLAs
- **API Read Latency (`NFR-PERF-02`):** Interactive read endpoints respond with `p95 <= 500ms`, `p99 <= 1.5s`.
- **API Write Latency (`NFR-PERF-02`):** Mutation endpoints respond with `p95 <= 800ms`.
- **Student Dashboard Load (`NFR-PERF-01`):** Complete rendering within `p95 <= 2.5s` on standard 4G broadband connections.
- **Candidate Search Performance (`NFR-PERF-03`):** Employer search with up to 5 facet filters executes within `p95 <= 2.0s` over 100,000 searchable profiles.
- **Asynchronous Execution (`NFR-PERF-04`):** Long-running tasks acknowledge status in `<= 1s`; AST repository scanning and skill inference complete in `p95 <= 15 min`; interview scoring completes in `p95 <= 30 min`.
- **Assessment Autosave (`NFR-PERF-06`):** Candidate answers committed to Redis drafts every 30 seconds; zero data loss on network reconnection.

### 2. Scalability Targets
- **Cohort Onboarding (`NFR-SCAL-01`):** System sustains 10,000 student bulk imports and 2,000 concurrent registrations per hour with `< 0.5%` error rate.
- **Concurrent Test Takers (`NFR-SCAL-02`):** Platform sustains 2,000 concurrent active examination sessions within agreed API latency targets.
- **Design Capacity (`NFR-SCAL-03`):** Database and multi-tenant schema engineered for 100 partner universities, 1,000 corporate employers, and 500,000 student profiles.

### 3. Reliability & Operations
- **System Availability (`NFR-REL-01`):** `>= 99.9%` monthly uptime across student, university, and employer portals.
- **Backup & Recovery Objectives (`NFR-REL-02`):** Recovery Point Objective (RPO) `<= 15 min`; Recovery Time Objective (RTO) `<= 4 hours`.
- **Idempotency Guarantee (`NFR-REL-03`):** Mutating HTTP calls support `Idempotency-Key: {UUIDv4}`; retries produce zero duplicate records, scores, or notifications.
- **Fault-Tolerant Retries (`NFR-REL-04`):** Failed BullMQ background jobs retry up to 5 times with exponential backoff before routing to Dead Letter Queues (DLQ); zero lost evidence items.

### 4. Privacy, Security & Responsible AI
- **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance (`NFR-SEC-01` / `SEC-01`):** No Aadhaar or PAN storage. Explicit, revocable candidate consent captured before camera/microphone activation.
- **Automated 30-Day Purge:** Webcam proctoring snapshots, audio transcripts, and interview telemetry tagged with 30-day retention TTL and permanently purged by automated cron workers.
- **Responsible AI Boundary (`SEC-02.06` & `SEC-02.09`):** Emotion recognition, facial-expression analysis, and personality inference are mechanically prohibited. Autonomous hiring rejections are banned—all final employment choices belong to human employers.
- **Audit Immutability (`ADM-05`):** Administrative and scoring audit events cannot be modified or deleted through application interfaces.
